1. Introduction

LikhDaal is a personal expense and income tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and your choices. By using LikhDaal, you agree to this policy.

2. Summary

  • Your financial entries are stored on your device, not on our servers (current version).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and local data from Settings.
  • We do not currently sync data to the cloud.

5. How we use information

We use data only to operate the app, secure access with MPIN, send local reminders you enable, and export reports you request. We do not use your data for advertising.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal or financial entries with third parties in the current version, except if required by law.

7. Your choices

You can access, update, or delete your data in the app. Use Settings → Delete account to remove your data from the device.

8. Children

LikhDaal is not directed at children under 13 (or under 18 for financial use).
